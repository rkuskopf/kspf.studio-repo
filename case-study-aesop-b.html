Architecture for Discovery

Aesop’s brand is built on a "less is more" philosophy. However, global performance data revealed a major friction point: users were dropping off at the very first step of the checkout journey. Surprisingly, Japan had the highest drop-off rates globally. The challenge was to diagnose why a minimalist interface—which traditionally reduces friction—was actually causing it in certain high-value markets.
